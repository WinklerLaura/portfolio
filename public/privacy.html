Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by World4You Internet Services GmbH, Wolfgang-Pauli-Straße 2, 4020 Linz, Austria, on servers in Austria. When you open a page, the server automatically processes technical data that your browser sends: your IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested, and your browser and operating system. Contact by email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. The legal basis is your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or my legitimate interest in answering it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete the email when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To use them, email me. You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), www.dsb.gv.at.
